# Selected identity

Direction 01 was selected and redrawn as a production vector. The mark uses four distinct stream tiles, a shared viewport, and one small live-status accent. The structure is balanced and remains identifiable when reduced to favicon size.

The participant-panel directions were rejected because they made the product look like a meeting client. The lane direction was rejected because it looked like a network or cable utility. The original weather-heavy icon is preserved under `originals/`, but it was too specific for the wider product.

Production files live in `assets/brand/`. Raster exports live in `assets/icons/`.
