# Multistreamer identity directions

## Direction 01: shared viewport

Four stream tiles converge on one shared player. This direction communicates the product in a compact shape and stays legible at small sizes. It became the basis for the production mark.

## Direction 02: participant panels

A grid of four portrait-style panes around a play symbol. It reads clearly, but feels closer to video conferencing than multi-source viewing.

## Direction 03: wordmark

A wide, lower-case wordmark with a bright stream accent. It works as supporting typography, but doesn't provide a distinctive app icon by itself.

## Direction 04: framed emblem

Four panes inside a rounded badge. The framing is polished, though the participant shapes still suggest meetings and calls.

## Direction 05: converging lanes

Parallel signal lanes merge into one destination. The metaphor is relevant, but the silhouette resembles a cable or network utility at small sizes.

## Production criteria

- Recognizable from 16 pixels through storefront artwork
- Neutral across watch parties, live events, monitoring, and production use
- Clear on dark and light backgrounds
- No text, version number, weather symbol, or platform logo in the app mark
- A restrained live indicator, not a generic red recording dot
